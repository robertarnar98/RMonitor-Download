# RMonitor

Live view and REC for several Sony cameras at once, over Wi-Fi, on your Mac. Every camera's picture sits side by side in one window, and one big **REC ALL** button starts and stops recording on all of them together. Each camera also has its own REC button, battery, card time and a red frame with a timer while it records. Click a camera to see it big, click again to go back to the grid. Rename cameras, rotate portrait ones, and go full screen. Save each set of cameras as a project, like "My Kit", and open it from the home page to connect to those cameras. **Sync Sound** (optional) plays a short beep from the Mac just after REC ALL starts, so every clip gets the same spike in its audio and lines up instantly in the edit.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/RMonitor-Download/releases/latest)**: click **RMonitor.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **RMonitor.dmg**.
2. Drag **RMonitor** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. RMonitor is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Connect a camera

Put the Mac and the cameras on the same Wi-Fi. On each camera, turn on **Remote Shooting** and **Access Authentication**. Then click **Add Camera** in RMonitor: it finds the cameras on your network, and you type the user and password the camera shows. **Help ▸ How to Connect** walks you through the camera menus step by step.

Tested with the Sony a7 V. Other Sony cameras that support Sony's Camera Remote SDK over Wi-Fi should work too.

## Updates

RMonitor doesn't check for updates. Come back to this page for new versions.

## Privacy

RMonitor never goes online. It only talks to your cameras on your own network. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Camera passwords stay in your Mac's Keychain, and footage stays on the camera cards.
